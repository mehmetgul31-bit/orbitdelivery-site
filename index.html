For independent takeaways in the UK

Your own online ordering and delivery, without the big-app commission.

Orbit Delivery gives your takeaway its own ordering page, a dashboard to run orders, and local riders to deliver them. A flat monthly price instead of a cut of every order.

Talk to usSee pricing

What shops get

Everything runs from one dashboard, on a tablet, laptop or phone.

Your own ordering page

Customers order and pay by card on a page with your shop’s name and menu. Payments go straight into your own Stripe account.

Order dashboard

New orders, printing, refunds, reports and a cash report, all in one place.

Riders on demand

Send a delivery to a nearby Orbit rider, or to your own drivers through the same app.

Safe handover

A 4-digit pickup code on each bag makes sure the right rider takes the right order.

How it works

STEP 1

Customer orders

From your ordering page, paying by card, or cash where the shop offers it.

STEP 2

You accept and cook

The order appears in your dashboard and prints with its pickup code.

STEP 3

A rider collects

An Orbit rider or your own driver accepts it, enters the code and delivers.

Pricing

Simple, published prices. No commission on your food sales.

Shop subscription

£29 per month
  • Ordering page, dashboard and reports
  • Paid monthly by Direct Debit
  • 30 days’ notice to cancel

Per delivery

50p per delivery
  • For each delivery sent through the Orbit app
  • Plus the rider’s pay when an Orbit rider delivers
  • Card fees are charged by Stripe to your account

Customer delivery fee

From £0 first 2 miles
  • Free, or from 49p to £2.99 for the first 2 miles
  • £1 per mile after that
  • Deliveries up to 4 miles

Orbit Delivery Ltd is not VAT-registered, so no VAT is added. See Refunds & cancellations.

For riders

Deliver for local takeaways as a self-employed rider using the Orbit Driver App. Choose the jobs you want.

£4.99 per delivery

For the first 2 miles, then £1.20 per mile.

Waiting time paid

15p per minute after the first 5 minutes at the shop.

100% of tips

Every penny customers tip goes to you.

Riders must have the right to work in the UK. See the Rider Terms.
