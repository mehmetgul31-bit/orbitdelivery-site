For independent takeaways in the UK

Your own online ordering and delivery, without the big-app commission.

Orbit Delivery gives your takeaway its own ordering page, a dashboard to run orders, and local riders to deliver them. Under £1 a day for your dashboard and just 50p a delivery — instead of a cut of every order.

7 days free · No contract · We set you up in person

What shops get

Everything runs from one dashboard, on a tablet, laptop or phone.

Your own ordering page

Customers order on a page with your shop’s name and menu, and pay by card — or cash, where you offer it. Your customers stay yours.

Order dashboard

New orders, printing, refunds, reports and a cash report, all in one place.

Your drivers, Orbit riders, or both

Use your own drivers through the Orbit app, local Orbit riders, or your drivers first and Orbit as back-up. Customers track their order live.

Safe handover

Every ticket prints with a scan code and a 4-digit code, so the right rider takes the right bag.

How it works

STEP 1

Customer orders

From your ordering page, paying by card, or cash where the shop offers it.

STEP 2

You accept and cook

The order appears on your kitchen board and prints with its scan code.

STEP 3

A rider collects

An Orbit rider or your own driver scans the ticket and delivers.

Pricing

Simple, published prices. No commission on your food sales.

Shop subscription

£29 per month — under £1 a day
  • Ordering page, dashboard and reports
  • 7 days free to try
  • No contract — month to month, stop any time
  • Paid monthly by Direct Debit
  • We come to your shop and set you up

Per delivery

50p per delivery
  • For each delivery sent through the Orbit app
  • Plus the rider’s pay when an Orbit rider delivers
  • Card fees are charged by Stripe to your account

Customer delivery fee

From £0 first 2 miles
  • Free, or from 49p to £2.99 for the first 2 miles
  • £1 per mile after that
  • Deliveries up to 4 miles
