Questions from shop owners

Do you take a percentage of my orders?

No. You pay £29 a month and 50p for each delivery through the app. Your food money is yours.

Can I keep using my own drivers?

Yes. Add them to the Orbit Driver App, or keep running them by hand. You decide how you pay them.

What happens if my driver is out and an order comes in?

Choose “Mine first, then Orbit”: your drivers get the job first, and if nobody takes it, a local Orbit rider is offered it.

Can customers pay cash?

Yes, where you allow it. New cash customers verify their phone number, and cash jobs go only to your own drivers or riders you trust with cash.

Do I need to set anything up myself?

No. Send us your menu and we build your shop, then visit to set up the printer and train you.

Is there a contract?

No. It's month to month by Direct Debit, and you can stop any time.

Do I need special equipment?

A tablet, laptop or phone with internet. A receipt printer is recommended; we'll advise which.
